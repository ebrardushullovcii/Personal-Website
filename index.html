How I work

AI adds speed. Judgment keeps the work honest.

I use agents and automation throughout delivery. The loop around the tools is what makes the result dependable.

  1. 01

    Start from the source of truth

    Code, live behaviour, logs, screenshots, and the people closest to the workflow, before any abstraction.

  2. 02

    Make the slice explicit

    Acceptance criteria, interfaces, and risks, then the smallest sequence of changes that proves the idea.

  3. 03

    Build with leverage

    AI agents and automation for exploration and implementation. Architecture, security, and product judgment stay hands-on.

  4. 04

    Verify the real outcome

    Focused tests, product inspection, and side-by-side screenshots for UI work until nothing visible is off.

  5. 05

    Leave the system clearer

    Documentation, decisions, and handoff context so the next person can continue without archaeology.
